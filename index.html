Reading PDFs on your phone is awful. I fixed it.

Flippy is a PDF and EPUB reader that flips like a real book, reads to you, and explains the parts you don't get.

Textbooks, court docs, scanned handouts, that 400-page manual you've been avoiding 📖

New in Flippy 5.3

A new look for Flippy

📚

A new library

Your books as covers. The one you are reading sits on top, with Read and Listen one tap away. Press and hold a book for more.

🎧

Listen, right in the middle

Tap your page to bring up the tools. Listen is in the middle, next to Contents and Ask AI. Every other tool is under More, each with its name.

🎙️

Ask Aloud, now in words

Ask about your PDF out loud. The answer is written out in large letters that light up as Flippy says them. Tap Open page to go to the page it used.

💬

A new AI chat

Start with Summarize or Study, or ask your own question. Each answer has Open page, Listen and Copy. Your conversation stays when you come back.

🎁

And more

  • Study has a fresh look, with your score as you go
  • Share a PDF from the library on your phone or tablet
  • Look up a word: a cleaner Define sheet
  • New Settings and reading stats
  • A softer page sound
Listen

Read aloud, offline too

A natural voice reads while a dot follows the words, from 0.75× to 2×. Download chapters and listen with no signal.

Understand

Explain anything

Highlight what lost you. The answer uses the actual text of your document, not a chatbot's guess.

Everything else

Built for real reading

Night Pages, scanned PDFs, automatic chapters, flashcards and quizzes, free sync (your files are never uploaded), 18 app languages.
